For Organizations & Companies

Staying in motion when reality keeps changing

Workload. Deadlines. Change. Burnout. Today's workplace demands that we respond fast, adapt, think differently — and keep working together even when the plan changes. We created an experiential activity for organizations that practices exactly these abilities — through movement, play, rhythm and human interaction.

Let's build an activity for your team Message us on WhatsApp

You can't control everything that happens.
You can practice how you respond to it.

In a world of overloaded task lists and tight deadlines, we're required to make fast decisions, change direction, deal with uncertainty, and stay attentive to the people around us. But these abilities don't only have to be explained.

They can be experienced and practiced.

What happens when the plan changes midway?

  • What do we do when what we planned no longer works?
  • How do we respond to a situation we didn't anticipate?
  • Do we cling to the first solution we found — or manage to see another option?
  • And how do we keep communicating and working together as pressure rises?

In our activity, participants meet these challenges in real time. Not through a slide deck, not through a lecture. Through the body, movement, and the encounter with another person.

Our tool

And this is where capoeira comes in

Capoeira is a game with no script. Two people enter the circle. Each arrives with their own abilities, ideas and plans — but neither knows exactly what the other will do a moment from now.

You have to observe. Listen. Respond. Initiate. Change direction. Spot an opportunity. Improvise. Sometimes you lead, sometimes you react. Sometimes your plan works — and sometimes, in an instant, you need to find another way.

This is exactly why we use capoeira as a tool for working with people and organizations. Not to turn your employees into capoeiristas — but to create an enjoyable, challenging and surprising space where they can experience and practice flexibility, adaptability, creativity and real-time communication.

After the circle

From the circle — back to work

The experience doesn't end with the movement. We connect what participants experienced during the activity to the reality they know from work:

  • What did I do when my plan didn't work?
  • How did I respond to change?
  • Was I able to improvise?
  • Did I really see and listen to the person in front of me?
  • Did I know how to let go of one solution and try another?
  • And what helped me keep acting within a dynamic situation?

This is how an unusual activity also becomes an opportunity to reflect on the way we function as individuals and as a team.

You don't need to know capoeira. Really.

The activity is designed for working people — not athletes, not capoeiristas. No prior experience, capoeira knowledge, or special fitness is required.

We tailor the nature of the activity to the participants, the organization, the number of people, and the goals defined together.

It can fit into a company day, team activity, bonding day, wellness event, management activity, conference, or a broader organizational process.

Who are we?

The Birimbau Association works to promote capoeira in Israel as a tool for movement, education, community, and personal and group development. Behind us are years of experience in coaching, leading groups, and working with diverse populations.

We take the knowledge accumulated within the world of capoeira and translate it into an experience relevant to the challenges facing people and organizations in the real world.
