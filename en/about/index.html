About us

Welcome! We're the ABADÁ Capoeira Israel family

The international organization dedicated to preserving, developing and spreading the martial art of capoeira, under founder Mestre Camisa.

400 years of history

Capoeira began developing roughly 400 years ago in Brazil, when enslaved people brought from Africa created it as a means of defense and cultural preservation. To disguise their training, they wove in dance movements, music and song — giving rise to a martial art that is also a complete cultural celebration.

Today capoeira is officially recognized by UNESCO as part of humanity's intangible cultural heritage.

From Yeruham to the Golan

ABADÁ Israel is a school with branches across the country — a home for practitioners of every age and ability, including people with disabilities. Everyone can join and grow within a supportive, social framework.

ABADÁ Israel's national teams proudly represent us at international events around the world.

What does a capoeira class look like?

🛡️ Defense & attack 01

Practicing base movements, kicks and evasions at a progressive pace.

🤸 Jumps & acrobatics 02

Building strength, flexibility and body coordination.

🎵 Music & singing 03

Getting to know the berimbau, atabaque, pandeiro and traditional songs.

🤝 Working together 04

Partner and group drills that build confidence and connection.

The grading system

Capoeira cords (belts)

The cord system is built from colors representing your progress in training. Each color connects to an element of nature and carries its own meaning on the capoeira path.
