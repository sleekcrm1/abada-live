What is capoeira

A martial art that's also dance, music and living history

Capoeira is a unique Brazilian martial art that emerged roughly 400 years ago, developed by enslaved Africans who wove dance and music into their self-defense training to disguise it. Today it's recognized by UNESCO as part of humanity's intangible cultural heritage.

FAQ

Everything you'd want to know before your first class

What exactly is capoeira?

A rhythmic martial art blending movement, music, singing and acrobatics. Its heart is the 'roda' — the circle where two players carry on a dialogue of movement, surrounded by song and live music.

Who is a capoeira class right for?

Everyone! No prior experience, special flexibility or fitness is required. Classes are tailored by age and level, from young kids to adults.

I'm not flexible — can I still start?

Absolutely. Flexibility is a result of regular practice, not a prerequisite. Every class includes warm-up and drills that build it gradually and safely.

What makes capoeira different from other martial arts?

Capoeira is martial art, dance, game, music and history all at once — all inside the roda, instead of direct confrontation.

What should I wear to my first class?

Comfortable pants and a t-shirt — training is done barefoot. Bring water and an open mind; after your first few classes you can order the official ABADÁ uniform.
